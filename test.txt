 Here's how to test each new feature through the frontend:

  1. AI Diagnostic Assistant

  1. Go to Dashboard → Tickets
  2. Click on any ticket row to open it
  3. Click the "AI Diagnostic" tab
  4. Type symptoms in the chat and click Send

  2. Photo Upload & Damage Assessment

  1. Go to Dashboard → Tickets
  2. Click on any ticket
  3. Click the "Photos" tab
  4. Drag & drop images or click to upload
  5. After upload, click a photo → "Analyze with AI"

  3. Auto-Ordering System

  1. Go to Dashboard → Inventory
  2. Click "Auto-Orders" button (top right)
  3. Three tabs available:
    - Pending - Approve/Reject auto-orders
    - History - View past orders
    - Rules - Configure auto-order rules per part

  Also visible on Dashboard home page as orange alert box if pending orders exist.

  4. Customer Quote Approval

  1. Go to Dashboard → Quotes
  2. Click on any quote row
  3. In the modal, scroll to "Customer Approval" section
  4. Click "Send for Approval" button
  5. Copy the approval link shown
  6. Open link in new tab to see customer approval page

  5. Repair Timeline

  1. Go to Dashboard → Tickets
  2. Click on any ticket
  3. Click the "Timeline" tab
  4. Click "Generate Estimate" button

  6. Warranty Claims

  1. Go to Dashboard → Warranty Claims (in left sidebar)
  2. Click "New Claim" to create
  3. Click any row to see details
  4. Use status buttons to update claim status

  7. Login Page Demo Button

  1. Go to login page (http://localhost:3000)
  2. Click "Fill Demo Credentials" button
  3. Credentials auto-fill, then click Sign In
